LANDMARK REFERENCE IMAGES - REQUIRED FILENAMES
===============================================

Place your reference images in this directory with these exact filenames:

Required Images:
  1. face_outline.png  - 17 facial outline points (jaw to temples)
  2. eyebrows.png      - 10 eyebrow points (5 per eyebrow)
  3. eyes.png          - 12 eye points (6 per eye)
  4. nose.png          - 9 nose points (bridge + nostrils)
  5. mouth.png         - 20 mouth points (outer 12 + inner 8)
  6. teeth.png         - 2 teeth points (upper + lower)
  7. tongue.png        - 3 tongue points (tip, center, back)
  8. ears.png          - 12 ear points (6 per ear)

Format: PNG or JPG
Recommended Size: 512x512 to 1024x1024 pixels

See README.md in this directory for detailed guidelines on creating these images.
